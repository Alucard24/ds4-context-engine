# Release 0.4.13

Coordinated patch release for `ds4-context-core`, `ds4-context-reference-adapter`, and `ds4-context-engine`.

## Changes

- Historical retrieval filters Italian/English grammar, acknowledgements, workflow and generic finish requests from inferred keywords/simple symbols. Stopword lookup folds case, accents and compatibility characters without rewriting literal evidence. Explicit backticks, paths, qualified identifiers, flags and quoted phrases remain searchable, as do UI terms such as `schermate`, `premere` and `input`.
- Lexical-only retrieval rows require a verified identifier, phrase or FTS term match. FTS order, role, recency and unused budget can rank evidence but cannot independently qualify it. Semantic candidates retain their separate eligibility; the distinct-topic bonus stays bounded.
- Managed planning records complete native excluded-group/message totals and at most 32 content-free group details, oversized-first: planner-generated IDs/positions, estimator-unit cost, message count, kind, oversized/predecessor flags and allowlisted exclusion/rescue reasons.
- `/context explain` and the default `/context excluded` show compact native-group diagnostics separately from the historical inventory. `/context excluded all` preserves explicit item-level provenance and truthful persisted-rollup labelling. Legacy, observer and fallback manifests default to at most 40 item details when group diagnostics are unavailable.
- `context.excluded_oversized_turn` adds at most eight oversized-group details and a truncation flag. Native group totals/details survive persisted excluded-item rollup independently.
- [ADR 073](../ADR/073-lexical-evidence-eligibility-and-native-group-diagnostics.md) records the decision and its limitations.

## Scope and limitations

Recent-tail/input budgets, contiguous-tail selection, immediate-predecessor rescue, tool-call/result atomicity, privacy enforcement, provider transport and provider-facing defaults are unchanged. `chars-v1` remains the default; BPE and auto-tuning remain opt-in. Genuinely oversized older turns can still be excluded even below the global message target and remain warnings, now attributable to a specific group. Retrieving a relevant excerpt does not restore the entire excluded turn.

Canonical Pi history is untouched; manifests remain rebuildable, non-authoritative projections. New diagnostic metadata contains no message bodies, canonical entry IDs, paths, tool arguments/results, provider bodies or credentials. Existing manifests without the optional diagnostic block remain usable.

Deterministic lexical retrieval is not a guarantee of semantic correctness for every conversation or language. This patch has synthetic local multi-turn coverage through real DS4/Pi hooks and SQLite retrieval, including 40 older generic reminders, topic recovery and topicless requests, plus atomic tool exchanges, rescue outcomes and a 68,000-item historical inventory/persisted rollup. It does not establish behavior on the reporting host, live provider calibration, headroom transitions or auto-tuning safety across provider turns. No additional provider call is made for this release.
